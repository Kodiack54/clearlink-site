Who are the Hatchlings?

Hatchlings of Heartland is a coloring-book world from ClearLink. Four little drakes, each with an element of their own, explore Heartland one page at a time. Every page is drawn with big, friendly lines, and the activity packs come with answer keys.

Terra

The earth drake

Terra's pages are woodland adventures: trails, trees, meadows and the creatures that live there.

New pages for Aqua are being drawn

Aqua

The water drake

Aqua's pages are ponds, streams and lakes, with plenty of splashing.

Pyra

The fire drake

Pyra's pages are camping trips: tents, campfires and starry nights.

Frost

The snow drake

Frost's pages are snow days: sleds, snow forts and frozen ponds.

How to print

  • 1. Download Save the PDF, or the ZIP if you prefer one PNG per page.
  • 2. Print US Letter (8.5 x 11 in), plain paper, "fit to page" off. Cardstock works well for crayons and markers.
  • 3. Color Print as many copies as your family or classroom needs.
