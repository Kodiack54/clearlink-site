ClearLink Media - Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

1. Who we are

ClearLink Media is an internal tool used by the owner of the XP Scout channels to publish our own gaming videos to our own social media accounts.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information from viewers or the public. When the account owner connects a platform account (such as TikTok), the platform provides an access token and basic profile information (account ID, display name and avatar). This is used only to publish videos to that account and to show which account is connected.

3. How we use it

Access tokens and basic profile information are used solely to upload and publish our videos and to display the connected account inside the tool. We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on our own private server and are accessible only to the account owner and authorized team members.

5. Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in ClearLink Media, or revoking access in the platform's settings, ends our access. On request we delete stored tokens and profile information.

6. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: dobbysgothissock@gmail.com
